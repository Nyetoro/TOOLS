The outreach playbook

  1. Deploy this tool once. Report links are built from your publicUrl in config.js. Until you deploy it and set that, links only work on your own computer. See the README.
  2. Build a list of 25. Any niche, one niche at a time — 25 schools, or 25 salons.
  3. Collect their details. Grab each business's website and WhatsApp number from their Instagram or Facebook page, note them in a spreadsheet, then import it on the bulk tab.
  4. Run the bulk audit. The worst scores, and anyone with no website at all, are your best prospects because they have the most to gain.
  5. Send the report, not a pitch. WhatsApp the share link plus one line: "I checked your site — here's your free report, no charge." The report sells; you don't have to.
  6. Track every lead. Mark each one Contacted on the lead list so you never message anyone twice. Update to Replied, Won or Lost as it progresses.
  7. Follow up once after three days with the single most expensive issue: "The one that costs you the most is that nothing on the site is clickable to call."
  8. Quote a fixed price to fix the top three items. Small, clear, fast.
This tool only reads publicly available pages, the same way a browser does. Keep it that way: audit a handful of sites at a time, and stop if anyone asks you to.
